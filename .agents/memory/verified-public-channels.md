---
name: Verified public channels
description: Constraint for publishing contact, privacy, and security routes in public project surfaces.
---

Public contact and security channels must be owner-confirmed and monitored before they are presented as launch-ready. If no verified destination exists, keep the limitation explicit rather than inventing an address or service.

**Why:** A plausible-looking mailbox can misroute privacy or vulnerability reports and creates a false sense of accountability.

**How to apply:** Ask the project owner for the destination and response expectation, then reuse the confirmed route consistently across contact copy, disclosure pages, and security.txt.
